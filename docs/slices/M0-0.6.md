# M0-0.6 — Cosmos seed

## Goal

`seed:cosmos` loads tenant zero from the discovery files — 192 machines by segment, 14 people, 19 active maintenance codes, lists, shifts, settings and glossary — and commits a seed report naming every conflict. Nothing is re-typed.

## Files

- `scripts/seed-cosmos.ts` — reads the three discovery files, transforms and inserts, returns the report.
- `docs/plan/seed-report.md` — the committed seed report.
- `packages/db/test/cosmos-seed.test.ts` — Testcontainers: run the seed and assert the counts.
- `docs/slices/M0-0.6.md` — this plan.

## Sources

- `docs/discovery/source/cosmos-platform-export-2026-10-28.json` — 191 machines with nameplate fields, people, codes, settings.
- `docs/discovery/bhavin-v3/live-config-2026-11-09.json` — 192 live ids, lists, shift windows, team shifts.
- `docs/discovery/bhavin-v3/glossary-2026-11-09.json` — 86 English corrections, 58 Gujarati terms.

## Rules

- Live ids and lists win; machine attributes come from the export; VC-57 has no attributes.
- Robot is its own segment; VC-57 belongs to CNC.
- Idempotent: the seed deletes the `cosmos` tenant (cascades) and re-inserts it in one transaction.
- The seed report records the 191-vs-192 difference, the 13-people-plus-1-vendor count, the inactive codes, the shift-3 question and the glossary caveats.

## Test cases

1. 192 machines, with segment counts CNC 125, Conventional 16, Process 17, Utility 30, DG 3, Robot 1.
2. 14 people (13 maintenance + 1 vendor).
3. 19 active maintenance codes and 3 inactive.
4. 2 shifts; 86 + 58 glossary terms; 6 segments.
5. A second run is idempotent (still 192 machines).

## Out of scope

Historical breakdowns (M4a), the ticket import (M1b), translations, people write screens.
