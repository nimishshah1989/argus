# Handoff — ticket 663 (INS-Cell Change Log takeaway and yardsticks)

## What I did

- `cell-change-log/adapter.ts`:
  - `takeawaysOf` runs `takeawayFor` over the moves grouped by destination cell (`movesByCell`; moves with no `toCell` left out, `name` = `cellChip` label, `value` = count). The line's tone is forced to `neutral` with `tagOf('neutral', t)`, since a move is not a loss.
  - `priorMonthOf(asOf)` gives the calendar month before the as-of month: its `YYYY-MM` key and its English month name via `Intl` (`en-GB`, "October 2026").
  - `kpisOf` now sets `yardstick` on both cards with `insight.yardstick.prior`: moves dated in that month, and distinct machines that moved in it. A month with none prints `0`.
  - `cellLogModel` fills `takeaways` (`{ moves }`) from the full move set.
- `cell-change-log/View.tsx`: draws `model.takeaways.moves` with the kit `Takeaway` above the moves table (inside the non-empty branch).
- `changes/663-cell-change-log-insight.md`: one line.
