# Handoff 658: INS-BD % and PM split takeaways and yardsticks

## What shipped

- `bd-pm/adapter.ts`: one takeaway per table, computed from the table's cell rows (PLANT TOTAL left out, value = last column), with the meanings in the ticket (`cell`, `bd-share`, `pm-share` high-is-worse; `bd-hours`, `pm-hours` high-is-worse and additive). `sectionOf(title)` maps the engine's table title to its section id.
- Tile yardsticks, all kit words, computed from the page's own figures: Actual Breakdown and PM Downtime as a share of their sum; Avg Available Hrs / Machine as a share of the annual hours its line names; Total Available Hrs as a share of machines × that annual figure (Indian grouping); Fleet Availability as 100% minus the mean of the cells' Total %; Total Machines `v2m.none`, with the `shortcut:` comment.
- `bd-pm/View.tsx`: `around` draws the matching section's `Takeaway` above its table. Prop type is now `BdPmModel`.
- `bd-pm/register.ts`: `model: BdPmModel`.
- Figures, labels, columns and tables are unchanged. No i18n keys added; every sentence comes from the kit (`insight.outlier`, `insight.concentration`, `insight.yardstick.share|average`) or `v2m.none`.
