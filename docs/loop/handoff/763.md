Translating was tried in the rescue and taken back: these sentences use single-brace `{name}` placeholders (filled by `fill`/`say`, as every v2 takeaway sentence does), and `packages/shared/test/catalogue.test.ts` ("leaves no half-translated Gujarati with stray Latin letters") reads a single-brace placeholder in a Gujarati value as a Latin leak: 31 failures. No other Gujarati catalogue translates a single-brace sentence; Team Focus and Forecast Lab leave theirs in English the same way. Translating them needs a Plan decision (the test's rule or the placeholder style), not a builder edit.
