# D7 — Namespaced message catalogues in `packages/shared`

Date: 13 November 2026 · Status: accepted (architect); detail in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`

## Decision

Catalogues move to `packages/shared/src/i18n/<namespace>.en.ts` and `.gu.ts`, under 250 lines each; `status`, `actions` and `common` are split per module or status group. `.tsx` keeps `i18next/no-literal-string`; in `.ts`, label fields are typed `MessageKey`. Tenant-entered Gujarati is stored in `ui_translation` and laid over the bundled catalogue; missing keys fall back to English and are listed for the admin. Nothing is machine-translated.

## Why

SPEC §9 puts all strings in `packages/shared`; the server needs them for push text and PDFs. `en.ts` is at the line cap and is edited by every screen, which blocks parallel work.

## Rejected

- One catalogue file.
- A string-literal lint for `.ts`: too many false positives; a type is exact.
