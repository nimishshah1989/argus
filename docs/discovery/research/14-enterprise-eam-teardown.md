# Research: enterprise EAM/APM teardown (28 Oct 2026)

Derived from the 110-row capability matrix (doc tab "Capability matrix", source rows in `matrix_rows.tsv` logic). "All seven" in the matrix is credited here to the six transactional EAM suites; AVEVA and GE (APM layers, counted together as the seventh) are credited only where named. A missing entry means not found in the sources checked, not proof of absence.

## What the enterprise suites have that matters to us
- **Strategy libraries:** IBM Reliability Strategies ships prebuilt failure modes/tasks for 800+ equipment types (worth verifying the current count); AVEVA and GE have similar libraries → our Phase 2 industry packs (CNC, press, compressor, injection moulding).
- **AI that suggests codes and similar jobs:** IBM Work Order Intelligence (problem codes), SAP similar-order recommendations, IFS/Oracle assistants → Phase 1 failure coding, Phase 2 Fix Finder.
- **Agents that act:** SAP Maintenance Planner Agent, Oracle maintenance agents, IFS agents → Phase 3 planning agent, always with human approval.
- **Inventory optimisation (intermittent demand):** IBM MRO Inventory Optimization, IFS → Phase 3.
- **Permit-to-work, isolation/LOTO:** SAP, Hexagon, IFS → LOTO Phase 2, permits Phase 3.
- **What we skip:** 3D/BIM, linear assets, invoice matching, management of change, computer-vision inspection, AI-generated FMEA (IBM's own paper reports 'more than half' correct — not good enough).
