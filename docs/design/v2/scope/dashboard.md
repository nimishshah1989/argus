# Coverage: Dashboard, v1 "Today" (`screens/v2/dashboard`)

Built 20-Nov-2026 from the v1 page (`Dashboard.tsx`, `TodayFigures.tsx`, `NeedsNow.tsx`, `FigureTile.tsx`, `figure-content.ts`, `today-change.ts`),
the 20 `dashboard.*` figures the engine sends (`packages/domain/src/analytics/computers.ts` 117 to 181, `dashboard-flags.ts`, `plant-events.ts`),
`GET /analytics/figures` (and the same reply for the date 30 days earlier), the desk's `GET /state` (what needs you now), the Action Center board,
`docs/spec/SPEC.md` ("Dashboard: KPI tiles, bubble chart ... label every figure with its period") and `docs/spec/PARITY.md`
(`m4a.dashboard`, `m4a.drill-down`), and the e2e specs `analytics.spec.ts`, `analytics-action-center.spec.ts`, `analytics-shell.spec.ts`.
It starts from the 38 items of `batch1.md` section 1 and adds what that audit did not list. The test runs the real engine over the frozen answer key
(`fixtures/answer-key/`), so 20 figures, 117 machines on the bubble chart and 26 machines on the trend test are the engine's own.

**The formulas.** The audit could not verify that the redesign kept v1's formulas. The old fixture invented its own ("Breakdown hours this month",
"Breakdown share"). This screen computes nothing: every number is the engine's, printed as v1 printed it, over the span v1 used (the engine's span is the
as-of year to date for fleet availability, plant breakdown percent and plant events; every record to date for the totals, averages, PM and no-downtime
hours; the register today for the six counts; 30 days for breakdown-free). Each card says which. The change is v1's own `changeOf` (a verdict for a rate,
a quiet amount for a running total, none for a count or across New Year for a year-to-date figure), against the same figure on the date 30 days earlier.

**Money.** Today carries no money in v1 and none here, so there is nothing to withhold from a role; a test asserts no rupee sign is printed.

## What the old fixture showed that we do not hold (left out, not invented)

Decisions with an hours-won and rupee value and a Decide button, "targets" and last-month bullet bars, a 12-month breakdown-and-PM line, time to repair and
time between failures as headline cards, "PM on time", a machine "idle, no job loaded", and a 12-week trend per machine. None has a source in the engine
for Today (targets, a value per decision and job data are not collected), none was in v1, and none is printed.
