# D0 design preview notes

## Question

Does the agreed Apple-inspired, white, icon-led direction feel clear and actionable on Floor and Desk?

## Chosen direction

One direction only: clarity, deference and depth through white surfaces, restrained neutral type and borders, deep-teal actions, and labelled red/amber/green status pairs. Lucide outline icons support rather than replace words. Targets are at least 52 px, keyboard focus is visible, and reduced-motion preferences are respected.

Floor progressively reveals machine, situation and problem before a fake `BD-0150` confirmation. Desk keeps condition and work together, moving from the Open list into a working ticket on phones and showing list plus detail on desktop. More actions and native disclosures preserve Pause/Transfer, timeline, people and figures without crowding the first view.

Every person, machine and ticket is synthetic. State lasts only in memory; no action is saved and no API request is needed.
