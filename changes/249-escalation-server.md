- Escalation (server): a missed acknowledge, restore, pause maximum or sign-off, or a critical machine still down, tells the people on the plant's ladder through push, once per step, with one `escalate` entry in the ticket's history; it repeats up to the rule's cap and stops when the clock stops, the machine runs, the rule is switched off or someone acknowledges it (`POST /api/v1/tickets/:id/acknowledge-escalation`). A transferred ticket gets a fresh acknowledge window and escalates if nobody picks it up.
