- Lockout before electrical work (server half): a work type or a breakdown category can be flagged "needs lockout" in the plant's lists. A ticket carrying a flagged work type, or whose stored report names a flagged breakdown category, refuses Start (409, code `lockout_required`) until the technician confirms the plant's lockout steps with one call, `POST /api/v1/tickets/:id/lockout-confirm`. The confirmation is a `lockout_confirmed` entry in the ticket history with who and when and the steps as worded then. The steps are a new settings list, `lockout_step` (English, Gujarati and Hindi, switch-off, audited); until the plant has its own, four starting steps apply. The ticket detail and every command reply carry `lockout` (the steps and who confirmed). A pause and resume do not ask again; a handed-over or restored ticket does. Tickets with no flag start exactly as before.
