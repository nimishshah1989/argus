- Lockout before electrical work (server half): a work type or a breakdown category can be flagged "needs lockout" in the plant's lists. On a flagged ticket each person confirms their own lockout before they Start or join it, and a resume needs everyone who will be working afterwards (the 409 lists who is missing): `POST /api/v1/tickets/:id/lockout-confirm` with every step, recorded in the ticket history with who and when and the steps as worded then; otherwise the command answers 409 `lockout_required` with the steps. A confirmation is cleared when the ticket is paused, handed over, closed, cancelled, restored or reopened after a disputed fix, or when that person leaves it. A confirmation covers the steps as they were when it was made: a step added later asks people to confirm again. Raising "other work" with "start now" on a flagged work type leaves the ticket open for its lockout. The steps are a settings list, `lockout_step` (English, Gujarati, Hindi, switch-off, audited), seeded once with four starting steps as ordinary rows; with every step switched off a flagged ticket cannot be started (`lockout_steps_missing`). The raise, ticket detail and command replies carry `lockout`. Tickets with no flag behave exactly as before. Fresh breakdowns (a machine stopped, support) are not gated yet: their category is only known at close.
