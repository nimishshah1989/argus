- Supervisors, technicians, heads and admins can now record a breakdown that already happened, with when it stopped, when work began and when the machine ran again. It becomes a closed ticket with its report, so old reports and downtime figures are complete. A period that overlaps another breakdown of the same machine is refused.
- An operator (and every other role that may raise a problem) can now tell the server that a support ticket on their machine has become a machine stopped; only a board login cannot. A closed or cancelled ticket can no longer be upgraded, and on a shared phone the person acting is the one named, as for every other action.
- Work requests now get their own WR numbers (WR-0001, WR-0002 ...) instead of using up the breakdown numbers (BD-...); each kind counts on its own.
- The older settings reads (the settings list, a list's entries, and the missing translations) are now for the maintenance head and admin only, like changing them; the floor and the desk read the plant configuration as before.
- A report filed in the night shift after midnight now carries the date the shift started (a stop at 00:15 on 11 March is the 10 March report), for tickets closed live and for recorded past breakdowns alike.
