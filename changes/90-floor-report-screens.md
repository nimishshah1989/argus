- The floor's "Report a problem" is rebuilt on the approved design: find the machine by typing it or by its group, say whether it has stopped or needs support, pick a quick problem and Send (four taps). The Sent screen shows the ticket number. A machine already reported opens its ticket instead of a second report, and a machine with an open support ticket can be changed to stopped (by maintenance today; operators wait on a decision). Other work (not a machine) and My complaints (Booked, In progress, Fixed) have their own screens, and a `?m=VC-17` link opens a machine directly.
