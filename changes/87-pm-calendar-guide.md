- The PM calendar and the Guide are rebuilt in the new look. The PM calendar shows its two compliance figures as tiles, the month with an icon and a word on every job (planned, open, crowded), and the crowded days and every job as tiles beside it. Every job a head can drag now also has a "Move to date" button: it opens a small panel with a date field, Enter moves the job, Escape cancels, focus returns to the job and the page says where it landed. The Guide keeps every word and anchor, with one heading, the contents list and a readable column.
