- The account sheet is now a proper sheet on the floor and the desk: who is signed in, Change person on a shared phone, language (English or Gujarati, kept for the phone), Alerts, Change password and Sign out. Alerts show Off, On, Blocked by the browser (with where to allow them and a Check again button) or Not available, and an On phone can send itself a test. Signing out asks first and switches the phone's alerts off. On a wide screen the sheet opens as a side panel from the name at the foot of the left menu.
