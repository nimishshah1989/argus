- The person on the duty roster for the shift now running, or the person a ticket is assigned to, can acknowledge it (`POST /api/v1/tickets/:id/acknowledge`). That stops the ticket's acknowledge clock and writes "Acknowledged" to its history. Anyone else is refused (403): not on the roster, a vendor-level line, an operator, the board, an admin without a line, or a shared phone with nobody chosen. On a shared phone only the person chosen on that phone counts, not a name sent with the request. A second acknowledge is a 409, and a late acknowledge keeps the breach that already fired.
