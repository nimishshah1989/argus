Three more screens have a new look at their old addresses, and the old pages are gone: BD % and PM split, Category and cell, and MTTR and MTBF (under Problems). Each opens with the answer, keeps every figure, chart and table the old page had, and makes every machine and cell a link that opens its detail in the side panel. "See the rows" opens every ticket behind the page in the side panel, however many there are, and an old link with the rows in it opens those rows. Charts keep their legend and a "Show the numbers" table under them. The fourteen machine tables of Category and cell are one table of all the machines that you can search and filter by category.
