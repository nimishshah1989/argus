- On a shared phone, every ticket command now acts as the person chosen on that phone, never as someone the request names: a different person is refused and nothing is written, so no one can act, be recorded or be credited as someone else. The same holds for registering a phone for push messages, and for who starts the work when a ticket is raised and started in one step. Every ticket reply also says whether the person using the phone may acknowledge it (`canAcknowledge`), so the screen can show the Acknowledge button only to them. A shared phone now offers, and accepts, only the people of the team it serves (floor phones production, maintenance phones maintenance, supervisor and head phones either, never a vendor), and an operator can no longer start work by raising a ticket or report a breakdown as someone else.
