- People and logins now have real settings screens: the list shows each person's role, cell, shift, login kind and last sign-in; an admin adds and edits people and logins, and switches a person off with a reason (their mobile number and devices clear, their name stays on past tickets). A mobile number that is not in international format is refused in the server's own words. Ticked people can change shift, sign out their devices or be switched off together, each confirmed with a reason and a count.
- The audit trail is now readable: an admin or the head filters it by area, person and day, sees every configuration and correction change with its reason and old and new values, and the admin exports the filtered trail as CSV.
