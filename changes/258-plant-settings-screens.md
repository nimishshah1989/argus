- An admin or head can now edit machines (cell, segment, criticality, status), hide or show groups and add or switch off shifts on new settings screens at `/settings/machines`, `/settings/groups` and `/settings/shifts`. On a phone it is a list with an edit sheet; at 1024 px and wider it is a sortable table, a left menu and an edit side panel. A cell change says it will be dated today and keeps past tickets in the old cell. All three screens are one `CrudPage` configuration each, so the lists, glossary, company and people screens reuse it.
