- The design preview has one finished reference screen, the analytics overview, at `/gallery?view=showcase` (design preview only, nothing in the product changes). It shows six figures with their months and a comparison with the month before, a trend with a crosshair and a detailed tooltip, a Pareto of downtime by cause, a machine by month heat map and a panel of small charts per cell, plus a side sheet that explains each figure. Pointing at a month on the trend lights that month in the heat map, the tile lines and the cell panel; pointing at (or tabbing to) a heat map square shows its month on the trend; pointing at a Pareto bar lists its machines in the tooltip and outlines those that are among the ten machines in the heat map, naming the rest. Every number comes from the engine's snapshot of 11 Nov 2026.
