- When production sends a fix back a second time (or more), the plant's "reopened twice" rule now tells its ladder (the head, by default) once, and it shows in the ticket's history like the other escalations. It is seeded switched off, so nothing changes at a plant until the head switches it on; with it off, nothing is sent. A rule switched on later does not reach back to tickets reopened before. It repeats and stops like the other rules (the ticket closes or is cancelled, the rule is switched off, someone acknowledges it).
