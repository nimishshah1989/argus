# 76g-76i — A1 reads many years

- Nothing a user sees changes yet. The BD % and PM split page (A1) can now be run for one span of plant dates. On the
  two-year test plant, 2025 prints July as "no records" (not 0.00%), counts 16,500 available hours (not 18,000) and
  shows the year at 0.40%; both years together print 24 months labelled with their year (Jan 25 to Dec 26), eight
  quarters and the title 2025–2026. A month with no records is left out of its quarter's average.
- A run that names no span is unchanged, so the 2026 answer key and every existing test stay as they were.
