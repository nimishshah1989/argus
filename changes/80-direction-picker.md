# 80l to 80n — the direction picker

- A design preview now opens at `/gallery?view=directions`. A small bar at the bottom flips the whole preview between the
  three directions (Instrument, Control Room, Ledger) with the keys 1, 2 and 3 or three buttons, and the address keeps
  the choice.
- The Elements screen shows each direction's tile shape, the way a change is written, Indian number grouping
  (10,00,000), where a chart's key sits, and the chart's frame, plus the other chart colours and the two colour ramps.
- Nothing else changes for users: the preview is for the owner to choose a look, and the real screens are untouched.
