- The live board's day score now counts only what the board shows: breakdowns on machines in a hidden group, or in a segment kept off the board, no longer move the Response and Repair tiles or the counts (not started, still open). A machine moved to another group counts by the group it is in now. The day score also says how many tickets each average is over, and how many of those are closed, so a screen can say "average of 6, 4 closed".
