- The Breakdown Log, the registers and the machine page are rebuilt in the new look. The Breakdown Log opens on one page of 50 rows out of its 1,343, newest first, with sorting, paging and the same four filters, row numbers and values as before. The registers show their counts and loads as tiles above their tables. A machine's page shows its age, floor area and loads as plain tiles, and its breakdown, repair-time and PM tiles open the rows behind them. Its nameplate, PM visits, record completeness, spares, tasks and moves sit beside or below. Gujarati words are added for all three.
