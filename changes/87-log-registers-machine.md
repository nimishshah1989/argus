- The Breakdown Log, the registers and the machine page are rebuilt in the new look. The Breakdown Log opens on one page of 50 rows out of its 1,343, newest first, with sorting, paging and the same four filters, row numbers and values as before. The registers show their counts and loads as tiles above their tables. A machine's page puts its age, floor area, loads, breakdowns, repair time and PM hours in tiles that open their rows, and its nameplate, PM visits, record completeness, spares, tasks and moves beside or below them. Gujarati words are added for all three.
