- After a maintenance fix, production can now sign it off. When a stopped or support ticket that someone else raised is closed, the person who reported it, any operator login in the cell the machine was reported in, or a supervisor, head, plant head or admin can confirm the fix or dispute it with a reason (`POST /api/v1/tickets/:id/handback`). A dispute reopens the same ticket for the last lead, who is told, and the machine counts as stopped again from then. A sign-off nobody answers within the plant's sign-off time shows as timed out; the ticket stays closed and a late answer is still taken. Every ticket reply now carries `handback`: its status and turnaround both ways (maintenance minutes, and production's wait plus its time to answer). Tickets closed before this change are not asked.
