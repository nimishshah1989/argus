Behind the scenes there is now one map that says, for each of the 51 figures, its unit and which direction is good, and for every status and severity its colour, icon and word. Shared helpers use it to print a change in the right colour, icon and word with `%` or `hrs` after it. A new check, `pnpm ui:check`, also reports screens that pick their own colours or build their own cards; it only warns for now.
