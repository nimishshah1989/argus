- The breakdown PDF's QR now names the machine by its code (for example `TC-7`), exactly as the old platform's QR did, so scanning a printed sheet finds the machine. Before, it held an internal id the old importer could not use. The day report now takes its day and night windows from the plant's own shifts (Cosmos: shift 1 08:00–18:30, shift 2 18:29–05:00) instead of a fixed list, follows a change made in plant settings, and with no shift chosen covers the whole calendar day (the desk's "Day report" used to mean the day shift, so it now shows more). "Pending at the end" for a past day is now judged from the ticket's history: it includes tickets that were open then and closed later, and leaves out ones cancelled then, even if restored afterwards. The QR keeps the machine code the ticket was raised under if the machine is renamed later. The plant head gets a clear "team day report is not available yet" instead of one person's report until the team report exists. Gujarati names and problems come out in both PDFs.
