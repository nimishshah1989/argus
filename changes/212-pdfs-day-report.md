- The breakdown PDF's QR now names the machine by its code (for example `TC-7`), exactly as the old platform's QR did, so scanning a printed sheet finds the machine. Before, it held an internal id the old importer could not use. The day report now takes its day and night windows from the plant's own shifts (Cosmos: shift 1 08:00–18:30, shift 2 18:29–05:00) instead of a fixed list, follows a change made in plant settings, and with no shift chosen covers the whole calendar day. "Pending at the end" for a past day now includes tickets that were open then and closed later. Gujarati names and problems come out in both PDFs.
