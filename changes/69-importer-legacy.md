- The importer now accepts what the 2022 to 2025 workbooks need. A utility failure on one plant date can load as one closed general ticket with a line per machine and its minutes, and it adds nothing to breakdown counts, downtime or repair time. Old breakdowns may carry a code that is switched off today, may sit on a machine that is no longer active, and may name a person by their `P-` code. A live ticket is still refused for a switched-off code or machine. Nobody sees a change on screen yet; the converter and the load come next.
