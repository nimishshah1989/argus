Five screens have a new look at their old addresses, and the old pages are gone: the Machines register, Age and Footprint (under Machines), and Fix Finder and Problem Analysis (under Breakdowns). Press the main figure on any of them and the machines or tickets behind it open in the side panel (the old "See the machines" and "See the rows"); press a machine in those rows and it opens on top, and Back returns to the rows.
