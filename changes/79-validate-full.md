- New command `pnpm validate:full`: it checks every figure on every ticket the plant holds, 2022 to today, and prints counts and figure keys only. It confirms each figure computes (the three money tiles may say "not computed yet"), that its drill rows sum to its number, that a second run and the kept run give the same answer, that the years add up to the whole span, and that every ticket is read by at least one figure. It exits non-zero while any difference is not listed with a cause in `docs/plan/validate-explained.md`.
