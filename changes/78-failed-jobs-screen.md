- The plant admin gets a Background jobs screen in Settings (English and Gujarati). It lists every job that failed, with what it was, when it failed, the reason and how many attempts, and a Retry button that runs the job once more. The Settings entry shows how many jobs have failed, with an icon and a word, and the list refreshes by itself, so a new failure shows within 30 seconds without a reload. Only the admin and the head on a personal login see it, the same rule as the server. The failed-jobs reply now checks that its times are real date-times. The head can now see the failed jobs and retry them too, as well as the admin; operators, technicians, supervisors, the plant head, the board and shared phones still cannot.
