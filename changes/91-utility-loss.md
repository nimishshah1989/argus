- Power and air failures now count as plant downtime. A new Dashboard figure, `dashboard.plant-events`, shows the machine-hours lost to them (each event opens from its ticket), and the fleet availability now includes those hours. They are never counted as breakdowns, so breakdown counts, MTTR and MTBF do not change. On the 2022 to 2025 history, 398 events took 2,720.5 machine-hours, and fleet availability over the four years went from 99.50 % to 99.34 %.
