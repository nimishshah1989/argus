- A head or admin can switch a machine to "not counted in production data" (the setting for spare-capacity machines), and back. Its breakdowns stay on record and keep counting in the manpower hours and the energy list, but no longer in BD %, MTTR, MTBF, availability or the other production figures. The figures say how many machines are left out. Every change leaves one line in the audit trail. No screen yet (77b).
