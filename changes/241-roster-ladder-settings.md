- The head (and admin) can now set the duty roster and the escalation ladder from generated settings screens: `/api/v1/roster` (who is on duty, per date, shift and level L1 to L4 or vendor; `active: false` takes a person off) and `/api/v1/escalation-rules` (what was missed, from which severity, which level is told, by push or WhatsApp, after how long, how often). A repeat under 15 minutes, a repeat with no cap (or a cap with no repeat), and a rule naming both a person and a role (or neither) are refused with a message on that field. A plant with no ladder starts with the agreed defaults (supervisor, head and plant head, push, repeats every 30 minutes at most 3 times) as ordinary editable rows; an edited ladder is never overwritten. Every change is audited. Roster lines older than a year are purged each night.
