# 76n — PM compliance and the reliability fit read the span

- Nothing a user sees changes yet: no screen names a span until ticket 75.
- The engine's PM compliance and reliability figures now read the span a run names instead of the as-of year. On the
  two-year test plant, PM compliance across both years counts all 28 PM work orders (28 of 28 on time) where it
  counted only 2026's 16, and the reliability fit starts at 1 January 2025 and counts TC-1's 60 failure dates where
  it counted 37.
- A month the plant kept no records in (July 2025) adds no working hours to a machine's reliability clock. The fitted
  shape and scale are ticket 67's and are untouched.
- A run that names no span is unchanged, so the 2026 answer key and every existing test stay as they were.
