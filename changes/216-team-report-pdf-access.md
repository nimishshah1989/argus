- The plant head now gets the team day report: one PDF for the whole maintenance team over the chosen shift or day, with each person's own minutes, a team total and the tickets still pending at the end (the old platform's "team day report"). Supervisors, heads and admins can ask for it with `team=1` as well as for one person. A plant head can no longer ask for one person's day. Breakdown PDFs now follow who may see them: an operator gets only the breakdowns they reported, a shared floor login gets its own cell's, maintenance, supervisors, heads, the plant head and admins get all, and the board login gets none.
